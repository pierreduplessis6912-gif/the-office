# Expenses (capability-map row "Expenses", Finance)

## What the ERPs do
- **A bill and an expense are different things.** Xero: a *bill* is a supplier invoice awaiting payment (accounts payable); *spend money* is a paid expense or receipt that goes straight to a bank transaction. QuickBooks: *bills* for unpaid invoices, *expenses* for paid purchases. Odoo: if something is already paid there is no payable, only a payment receipt, and the expense posts only to profit-and-loss accounts (https://support.getapron.com/en/articles/10145908-choose-expense-vs-spend-money-options ; https://developers.apideck.com/guides/expenses-bills ; https://www.odoo.com/forum/help-1/re-recording-a-vendor-bill-expense-that-s-already-paid-234646 ). The engine already has the bill side (supplier invoices, the procurement row); this row is the **paid expense**.
- **Every expense carries a category and a receipt**, and a tax code per line; split expenses are separate lines (Odoo, Xero, QuickBooks). Receipts are attached to the transaction (https://www.odoo.com/documentation/16.0/applications/finance/expenses.html ).
## What SARS requires for the input tax on an expense
- No input tax deduction unless the vendor holds a **valid tax invoice** (VAT Act s16(2)); a full tax invoice shows the supplier's name, address and VAT registration number, the recipient's details, a description, the date and a serial number (s20(4); https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/tax-invoices/ ; Grant Thornton, Feb 2026: https://www.grantthornton.co.za/insights2/tax-focus---february-2026-issue/ ). Above R5,000 the recipient's VAT number must also appear where the recipient is a vendor (https://www.vatupdate.com/?p=163845 ).
- If the supplier is **not a registered vendor** the VAT shown is invalid and cannot be claimed, even if charged in error (Grant Thornton, above).
- Claims must be made within **five years** of the tax invoice date and the proof kept five years (VAT 404 guide: https://www.sars.gov.za/legal-pub-guide-vat404-vat-404-guide-for-vendors ; SARS FAQ: https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/value-added-tax/schools-exiting-the-vat-system-frequently-asked-questions/ ).
- **Denied outright** (s17(2)): entertainment, motor cars (acquisition) and sports, recreational and social club subscriptions, even when used for taxable supplies (https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/vatguide0.pdf ; Forvis Mazars: https://forvismazars.com/za/en/who-we-are/news-and-publications/our-publications/forvis-mazars-messenger/mazars-messenger-november-2020/5-common-tax-pitfalls-small-business-should-avoid ).
- VAT201: input tax is field 14 (capital goods and services supplied to you) and field 15 (other goods and services); field 19 totals the input tax (https://www.sars.gov.za/smme-value-added-tax-detailed-completion-of-vat-201 ).

## Decisions (defaults)
1. **An expense is a paid purchase.** `expense.record` is a held write that records, in one transaction, an `expense` document (direction buy, from a supplier) and the **money-out movement** that paid it, plus an `expense.recorded` event tying them. There is no payable (`expense` is not a payable kind) and no allocation. An unpaid supplier invoice is a bill (the procurement row), not an expense.
2. **Category is required and comes from a fixed list** (advertising, bank_charges, cleaning, consumables, entertainment, fuel, insurance, materials, motor_car, office_supplies, professional_fees, rent, repairs, stationery, subscriptions, club_subscription, telephone, tools, travel, utilities, other). A fixed list is what lets the engine apply the s17(2) denial reliably; `other` is allowed and flagged for classification. A business-specific list is a later decision.
3. **Tax is explicit per line, never assumed**, and the entry says whether the entered amounts include tax (`amountsIncludeTax`, required). Tax is computed per line by the engine; it can differ by a cent from a supplier's invoice that rounds at invoice level, so the **amount actually paid** must equal the computed total within one cent per line, or the expense is refused (it catches typos and mis-keyed tax).
4. **Input tax claimability is derived, never typed**: claimable only if the business is VAT registered (profile), the supplier is a vendor (a valid 10-digit VAT number on the supplier's record), a valid tax invoice is held (`taxInvoiceHeld`; a purchase of R50 or less needs only the receipt), the line's tax is above zero, and the category is not denied. The reasons are shown on the record. A purchase of capital goods (`capital`) goes to field 14, anything else to field 15.
5. **The VAT claim date is the invoice date, or the first open day if that period is locked.** SARS allows a claim up to five years later, so an expense dated in a locked period is allowed and its input tax falls in the first open period (Odoo's rule, which fits here); the VAT summary uses that date.
6. **No edit; correction is void and re-record.** Facts are never edited. `expense.void` (held, reason required) reverses the movement and the document; refused if its claim date is on or before the tax lock (it would change a filed return). Voiding does not delete anything.
7. **Duplicates:** the same supplier invoice number from the same supplier cannot be recorded twice (refused as `duplicate`).
8. **Receipts:** the engine stores a `receiptRef` (a reference to where the receipt image is kept); file storage itself is a separate capability, not built. Whether the document held is a valid tax invoice is the person's statement (`taxInvoiceHeld`), approved by a person, not something the engine can verify.
9. **Permissions:** `expense.record` and `expense.void` for the money roles (owner, accountant, admin: `can_manage_invoices`); reading expenses for `can_know_expense_totals` and the money roles. **Installers and salespeople cannot record expenses**, even though in practice they buy materials: letting field staff submit for approval needs a separate "submit" permission, a later decision. Receipts by voice note and the model are not wired.
10. **The VAT summary gets its input side** from recorded expenses (fields 14 and 15, net VAT); expenses without a valid claim are listed with their reasons. Supplier bills (procurement) will feed it later; until then the summary says input tax covers expenses only.

## Not in this capability (each a separate decision, none assumed)
Supplier bills and their payment (procurement), employee expense claims and reimbursement, receipt file storage and OCR, bank-feed matching, expense approval limits, project/job costing, a business-defined category list, mileage, foreign-currency expenses and imported services (field 16), and bad-debt or change-in-use adjustments.

## Findings from building it
- **The claim date is stored when the expense is recorded, not derived later.** An early design derived it from the tax lock at read time; that would have moved an October claim into November the moment October was locked. The claim date is now in the `expense.recorded` event.
- **The existing `credit.applied` / `payment.allocated` name clashes recur:** the new events are `expense.recorded` and `expense.voided`; no clash.
- **Open for Pierre or an accountant:** whether field staff should be able to submit expenses for approval (not built); whether the fixed category list fits the business; and the questions added to `docs/ACCOUNTANT_REVIEW.md` (questions 13 to 16).
