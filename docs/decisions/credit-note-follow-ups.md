# The four points, how other ERPs handle them, and what The Office does

Pierre's instruction (2026-11-10): close the four points left open after credit notes, researching how other ERPs do it. The four were: (1) the vendor's own particulars on a tax document, (2) VAT periods, (3) abridged invoices and rounding, (4) the credit-note actions not yet built (apply leftover credit, refund, void, debit note).

## 1. The vendor's own particulars (name, address, VAT number) on a tax document
**What ERPs do.** Xero, Sage, Odoo and Manager.io keep the organisation's own legal details and tax number in organisation settings and print them on every document; the "Tax Invoice" heading is conditional on the business being tax registered (Manager.io: https://forum.manager.io/t/invoice-template-not-showing-in-settings/2678 ; Xero SA guide: https://www.xero.com/za/resources/accounting-glossary/s/what-is-an-invoice ). Xero stores the customer's tax number on the contact and prints it as "Tax Number" (users ask for it to say "VAT Number": https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967115-invoices-quotes/suggestions/51658405-fix-sales-invoice-customer-vat-number-changed-t ). Xero's SA add-on XBert flags documents lacking a supplier VAT number rather than blocking them (https://support.xbert.io/en/articles/11078913-vat-has-been-claimed-on-a-bill-without-a-vat-number-on-attachment-za ).
**South African rules** (VAT Act s20 and s21(3), as set out by SARS and BDO; the three-tier summary is from a secondary guide, https://invoicedataextraction.com/blog/south-africa-vat-invoice-requirements , whose core claims match the SARS rulings): a credit note must show the words "credit note", the vendor's name, address and VAT number, the recipient's name and address (and VAT number if a vendor), the date, the reduced value and the excess tax, a brief explanation, and the identity of the original transaction. The vendor's VAT number is 10 digits beginning with 4. The Companies Act s32(4) additionally requires a company's registered name and registration number on its documents (same guide).
**Conclusion.** Add a **business profile** to the engine (legal name, address, VAT registration status and number, company registration number), set through a held write by the owner, and a **recipient VAT number** on customers and suppliers. Do **not** block issuing a credit note or invoice when the profile is incomplete (no ERP above blocks; they flag): instead derive a **document completeness check** that lists exactly which prescribed particulars are missing, show it on the record, and make it the gate for any future send/PDF ("must not be sent as a tax document until complete").

## 2. VAT periods
**What ERPs do.** Odoo's *tax lock date*: a new transaction dated before the lock has its tax moved to the next open tax period; a *hard lock* can never be undone; a locked invoice is corrected only by a credit note in the open period (https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/finance/accounting/reporting/tax_returns.html ; https://www.odoo.com/forum/help-1/odoo-18-lock-dates-frequently-asked-questions-263564 ). Xero's *lock date* stops anyone adding or editing transactions dated on or before it (advisers can override); users repeatedly ask for a separate VAT lock distinct from the accounting lock, and for a credit to be allocated with its OWN date in the open period instead of being blocked (https://help.omni.cin7.com/hc/en-us/articles/9128588040975-Xero-lock-date ; https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967136-banking-chart-of-accounts/suggestions/49408478-lock-date-separate-lock-dates-for-sales-tax-and ; https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967139-purchase-orders-bills-inventory/suggestions/44960803-lock-dates-allocate-credit-notes-to-invoices-bil ). SAP posts into the next open period while the VAT report can still pick the document up by its own date (https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/217032 ).
**South African rule.** The credit note's date is its issue date and the adjustment falls in the period it is issued (Business Day, https://www.pressreader.com/south-africa/business-day/20240513/282106346736189 ); s21 sets no deadline between invoice and credit note (https://www.moonstone.co.za/?p=59982 ).
**Conclusion.** A **tax lock date** per business, moved only FORWARD by a held write from the owner (Odoo's hard-lock principle; unlocking is a deliberate accountant-level act that is not built). Tax documents (invoices and credit notes) must be dated **after** the lock: refused with a message to date them in the open period. This is the Xero behaviour, chosen over Odoo's silent re-dating because SA law dates the adjustment by the issue date and silently changing a document's date would falsify it. **Applying an existing credit to an invoice carries its own date and is NOT blocked by the tax lock**, because it changes no VAT (the Xero complaint, adopted). Voiding a credit note dated on or before the lock is refused (it would change a filed return).

## 3. Abridged invoices and rounding
**Abridged.** SARS allows an abridged tax invoice for supplies up to R5,000 (omitting the recipient's name, address and VAT number), nothing formal at R50 or less, and a full tax invoice above R5,000; a supplier may always issue a full one; BGR 72 applies the same recipient relief to credit notes for such supplies (https://www.sars.gov.za/legal-intr-r-bgr-72-particulars-to-be-contained-in-a-credit-note-for-a-valid-deduction-under-section-163av-for-prepaid-vouchers ; tiers: the secondary guide above).
**Rounding.** Odoo offers "round per line" and "round globally" and its own help says to choose per line when selling tax-inclusive (https://www.odoo.com/zh_TW/forum/支援-1/round-only-total-in-invoice-19895 ); Xero rounds per line and sums (https://help.file.ai/xero-rounding-issues ), which users note can differ by cents from the arithmetic total. Neither method is wrong; the controls that matter are consistency and that a credit cannot exceed what was charged.
**Conclusion.** The engine **always issues full tax documents** (every document has a customer), which SARS permits at any value, so no abridged tier is built; the completeness check requires the recipient's name and address when the document exceeds R5,000 and the recipient's VAT number when the customer has one on record. **Rounding stays per line** (as invoices already do) and two rules are enforced and tested: a full credit reproduces the invoice's tax to the cent for any lines, and a run of partial credits may **never credit more tax on a line than the invoice charged** (a credit that would, by rounding, is refused with an instruction to credit the line in fewer notes).

## 4. The credit-note actions not yet built
**What ERPs do.** *Refund*: a credit note reduces what is owed; returning cash is a separate payment recorded afterwards (Odoo: "register the payment afterward if you need to send money back", https://www.odoo.com/documentation/user/13.0/accounting/receivables/customer_invoices/credit_notes.html ; Xero: apply the credit note then pay the refund from the bank, https://help.1stformations.co.uk/accounting-service/recording-a-customer-refund-or-credit-note ). *Apply to another invoice*: a leftover credit is allocated to a later invoice, partially or in full (QuickBooks, Xero, Manager.io; https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-sg/manage-customers-and-income/enter-and-apply-a-credit-note-or-delayed-credit/01/382841 ). *Void*: a credit note that has been allocated or refunded cannot be voided until those allocations are removed (https://www.xenonconnect.com/?p=2284 ; https://help.re-leased.com/hc/en-au/articles/25242785640468-Editing-and-Voiding-Credit-Notes ). *Debit note*: the only legal way to increase an invoice already issued is a debit note referencing it (Odoo: https://www.odoo.com/documentation/master/applications/finance/accounting/customer_invoices/credit_notes.html ; SA: s21 debit notes, same guide as above).
**Conclusion.** Four held writes, each following those rules: **`credit.apply`** (allocate unapplied credit to another outstanding invoice of the same customer, with its own date, never more than the credit left or the invoice owes); **`refund.record`** (money out to the customer, never more than the credit left, a separate movement and a `credit.refunded` event); **`credit_note.void`** (reverse the credit note, its allocation and its movement together; refused once any of its credit has been applied elsewhere or refunded, or if dated on or before the tax lock; the invoice's balance returns); **`debit_note.issue`** (a document of kind `debit_note` increasing an invoice's charges, lines inheriting the invoice line's tax rate, owed by the customer like an invoice and so a receivable kind).

## Findings from building these four (2026-11-10)
- **The leftover credit is now fully traceable.** A credit note's leftover is the movement not yet allocated, less refunds (events); `receivables.query` and the customer record show it net of refunds; `credits.find` (`GET /engine/credits?partyId=`) lists credit notes that still have credit.
- **A refund and an application cannot both spend the same credit**: each checks what is left, and the verifier reports any credit note whose applications and refunds together exceed its total.
- **Unapplying an application is not built.** A credit note whose credit has been applied or refunded therefore cannot be voided (the ERPs above also make you remove allocations first); undoing an application or a refund is a separate, later decision.
- **Refunding an overpayment** (not a credit note) is not built; `refund.record` is for credit notes only.
- **No staging run yet** for any of the follow-ups; each would need its own proof mode, on Pierre's word.
