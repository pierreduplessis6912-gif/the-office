# Inventory (capability-map row "Stock: receive, issue, count, reserve, value, price list")

## 1. What best-practice ERPs do (secondary sources; the practice is consistent across them)
- **Master data first.** An item master (SKU or barcode, description, unit of measure with conversions, costing method, lot or serial flags, replenishment policy) and a location master (warehouses, bins, vehicles, in-transit) ( https://www.cleverence.com/articles/for-business/features-of-erp-inventory-management-system-4827/ ; https://www.cleverence.com/articles/for-business/inventory-management-module-in-erp-4827/ ). Retrofitting costing and tracking rules later is painful, so they are decided up front (https://www.cleverence.com/amp/articles/for-business/erp-inventory-module-6382/ ).
- **One ledger of movements.** Every receipt, issue, transfer, adjustment and count posts as a movement (debits and credits that reconcile to the financial ledger); quantity on hand, reserved and available-to-promise are derived from it; mistakes are corrected by further movements, never by editing ( https://www.cleverence.com/amp/articles/for-business/erp-system-inventory-control-4829/ ).
- **Valuation layers.** FIFO, weighted (moving) average or standard cost; the method is chosen once and kept; cost adjustments must be traceable; stock valuation must reconcile to the ledger ( https://www.cleverence.com/articles/for-business/inventory-management-module-in-erp-4827/ ).
- **Controls that stop damage:** no negative stock, no over-receipt, authorised and explained adjustments and count variances, reservations against open orders, exception reports (negative stock, valuation anomalies) ( https://www.cleverence.com/articles/for-business/inventory-module-in-erp-4732/ ).
- **Planning:** reorder points (min/max), safety stock, lead times, preferred supplier; replenishment turns a shortage into a purchase order (same sources).
- **Traceability:** lot and serial numbers for recall, warranty and expiry (FEFO); cycle counts and wall-to-wall counts with variance reports (https://erpresearch.com/erp-modules/inventory-management).

## 2. What South Africa adds (VAT Act is not involved; the Income Tax Act is)
- **Closing stock is valued at cost price less diminution** (s22(1)(a)): damage, deterioration, change of fashion, a fall in market value, or any other reason satisfactory to SARS. IAS 2 measures stock at the lower of cost and net realisable value (NRV) and **does not allow LIFO**; the SCA confirmed taxpayers choose between average cost and FIFO, and that an internal write-down *policy* is not enough without evidence ( https://www.sars.gov.za/lapd-drj-sca-2018-08-csars-vs-volkswagen-sa-pty-ltd-19-september-2018 ; https://www.sars.gov.za/lapd-drj-sca-2019-04-csars-v-atlas-copco-south-africa-pty-ltd-27-september-2019 ; SARS Interpretation Note 140, 16 October 2025: https://www.acts.co.za/sars-in-140-diminution-value-closing-stock/ ; https://bowmanslaw.com/insights/good-news-for-taxpayers-holding-obsolete-closing-stock/ ).
- **What this means for the engine:** only **weighted average and FIFO** are offered (no LIFO); a **write-down is a recorded movement with a reason and a note of the evidence**, never a silent edit; a **closing stock valuation as at a date** (cost less recorded diminution, by item) is a derived report an accountant can start from. Which write-down is just and reasonable is the accountant's and SARS's call, not the engine's.

## 3. THE DECISION THAT NEEDS PIERRE: how stock is stored
Everything built so far has deliberately made **no schema change** (facts and events only, everything else derived). Stock differs: items and locations are **entities with their own identity**, and the movement ledger is the **highest-volume** data in the business. Two honest options:
- **A. An additive migration (recommended):** three new tables, `eng_item`, `eng_location` and `eng_stock_move`, written exactly as the foundation tables were: immutable rows (update and delete refused by trigger), each tied to its event, foreign keys to `eng_document` for the receipt or invoice a movement came from, CHECK constraints (quantity above zero, kind from a fixed list), indexes for on-hand per item and location. Mutable attributes (name, reorder level, active) are events, derived as party profiles are. **No existing table changes shape** (the foundation STOP RULE holds); `FACT_TABLES`, the integrity verifier, the ownership test and the snapshot and restore (which enumerates tables generically) are extended deliberately. This is the shape the paper walk already proved fits (`tf2_item`, `tf2_stock_move`, `tools/engine-fit-domains.test.js`).
- **B. No migration:** stock movements as events, items identified by the id of the event that created them, every on-hand and valuation answer derived by scanning stock events. It keeps the "no schema change" record, but an item's identity would be an event number, the scans grow with history, and database constraints (no negative, valid kind) could not back up the code. Workable for a toy; **not robust** for the category Pierre asked to be robust.
- **The gate on record:** on 2026-11-01 Pierre signed that **no schema or stored-data change** happens until three successful synthetic restore drills. The staging restore proof passed on 2026-11-08 (`ops/staging-restore-proof-last.json`) and the restore drill had 11 passes in October, then a memory-limit failure at about 41,500 rows on 28 October that the later work addressed; whether that satisfies his gate is **his** call. **No migration is written, no table is created, and nothing is deployed until he says so.**

## 4. The capability catalogue, in the order it would be built (each increment: decisions, tests first, deliberate breakages, then the engine, MCP and recovery boxes, like every row before it)
1. **Foundation of the ledger.** `item.create`, `item.revise` (name, unit, reorder level and quantity, preferred supplier, active), `location.create` (warehouse, vehicle, site, in-transit), `stock.receive` (from a goods receipt, with the cost from that receipt line, or by hand with a cost), `stock.issue` (for a job, a sale, or use), `stock.transfer` (between locations), `stock.adjust` (damage, loss, found; a reason from a fixed list is required), `stock.move.reverse`, and the reads `items.find`, `stock.on_hand` (per item and location: on hand, average cost, value) and `stock.ledger` (every movement of an item, with where it came from).
2. **Control.** `stock.count` (a stocktake of a location: the engine works the variance against what the book says, shows it to the approver, and records count adjustments with the reason "stocktake"), reservations (`stock.reserve` and `stock.release` against a quotation or a job, so available = on hand less reserved) and reorder suggestions (`stock.reorder_suggestions`: items at or below their reorder level, with a suggested quantity and preferred supplier, ready to become a purchase order).
3. **Valuation and tax.** `stock.write_down` (a diminution with a reason and a note of the evidence), the closing-stock valuation as at a date, a FIFO option beside weighted average (chosen once per business; changing it needs an accountant's approval), and the stock-to-ledger reconciliation report.
4. **Traceability and pricing.** Lot and serial numbers (recall, warranty, expiry), unit-of-measure conversions (a roll of vinyl as metres or square metres), cut-to-size remnants, the price list (selling price per item, changing over time, with who changed it and why) and quotation lines that draw from it.
5. **Reach.** The interpreter ("issue three sheets to the Mokoena job"), the staging proof, and the app (a stock room, a count screen on the phone), each on Pierre's word.

## 5. The invariants that make it robust (each becomes a test, a deliberate breakage and a verifier check)
- **Stock can never go negative**, per item and per location; an issue larger than what is there is refused and says what is.
- **Movements are immutable.** A mistake is corrected by a reversing movement (once only, and refused if later movements depend on it) or an adjustment, never by an edit or a delete.
- **Every movement is explained**: what was said, what was understood, who approved it, which proposal, and where it came from (a goods receipt line, an invoice, a count, a job).
- **Cost is stamped when a movement is recorded and never changes after:** an issue carries the weighted average at that moment; back-dating or a later cost correction can never silently rewrite past cost of sales. When an item's quantity reaches zero its value reaches exactly zero (no rounding residue).
- **Traceable to the purchase chain:** stock received from a goods receipt line cannot exceed what that line received, so ordered, received, stocked and billed can be followed end to end.
- **Valuation reconciles:** the value of stock on hand equals the sum of its movements' values, per item; a transfer moves quantity between locations without moving value.
- **One writer, one transaction:** a movement and its event are written together or not at all (the atomicity test fails at every write), concurrent approvals are protected by version (two people cannot both issue the last of a line).
- **The verifier reports** orphaned movements, a movement whose event is missing or wrong, negative stock, value that does not equal the sum of movements, a reversal that does not mirror its original, and receipts beyond their goods-receipt line.
- **Recovery:** snapshot and restore bring every table, sequence and derived answer back exactly (`tools/engine-restore-stack.test.js` is extended with a stock history).

## 6. Defaults proposed (open to Pierre)
Weighted average first, FIFO in increment 3, **LIFO never** (IAS 2 and the SCA); quantities are fractional (thousandths) because flooring and blinds are sold by the metre and the square metre; a negative on hand is never allowed, not even by an adjustment; an adjustment always needs a reason from a fixed list (damage, loss, theft, found, correction, stocktake, write_off); a receipt takes its cost from the goods-receipt line (the order's price) unless a person gives a cost; the person on site may PROPOSE a movement and a money role approves it (as for goods received); stock value is **cost**, never selling price.

## 7. What is not in scope (each a separate decision)
Manufacturing and bills of materials (cutting a roll into pieces is a later "remnant" decision), landed cost allocation (freight and duty onto cost), consignment and drop-ship, bin and pick-path warehouse management, barcode and RFID scanning, multi-currency stock, and the general-ledger postings (the engine has no chart of accounts yet; valuation is reported, not posted).
