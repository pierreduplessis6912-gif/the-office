# System audit, Stage A part 1 (2026-10-31)

Work in progress: Stage A part 1 of the audit.

Read-only. Audited commit: `42bcb74` (main; `6aff562` plus two docs-only commits). Production runs `8738923` (HANDOVER State, not re-verified today). Nothing was changed in source, schema, workflows or production data. Order agreed with Pierre: audit the baseline, find gaps in current and proposed capabilities, build them, then the conversation and context layers, then the UX (`The_Office___the_orb_that_stays`, a mockup with invented data: one persistent orb, home, a list, one document, questions scoped to where you are, every answer tagged with its source, drafts confirmed by the owner). Evidence classes used: V-code (read in source), V-test (an automated test covers it), V-prod (checked in production), D (documented only), U (unknown). Everything below is V-code unless stated.

Storage boundary, the schema and cross-table links, the lead, snag and capture records. NOT yet covered: the request trace (capture to response), identity and context code, permissions, failure and recovery, business arithmetic, the customer journey against the code, tests against operating conditions.

| F-003 | The lead to customer link is made by NAME at quote time (`UPDATE leads ... WHERE name = ? COLLATE NOCASE AND status='enquired'`); a lead is created with no customer. So it is weakly connected, not unconnected: the earlier "disconnected" wording is too strong, and "by name" is weaker than the identity rules used elsewhere. | `finance.ts:2312, 2340, 2351` | Medium | Verified |
| F-004 | Two job-like tables exist: `jobs` (customer, quotation, status) and `job_scopes` (customer, installer, capture, project). Quotations and invoices link to `job_scope_id`. Which table carries which part of the workflow is not yet established. | baseline schema | Unknown | Open |
