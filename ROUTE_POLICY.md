# Route policy: who can call what

Generated by `node tools/route-policy-gen.js` from the **real** authentication gate, and checked on every build: the build fails if the gate behaves differently from this file, or if a route exists that is not in it. Do not edit by hand. To change who can do something, change the code, run the generator, and review the diff in this file.

Shown for the **default** role permissions; the owner can change them in Settings, which changes what an accountant or installer may do. Admin routes are not listed here: they have their own tiers (`worker/src/admin-routes.ts`) and need the admin key.

Classes: **public** 22, **any member** 13, **some roles** 27, **signed link** 6, **owner only** 32. A **signed link** route is opened only by a link the business sent (it carries its own signature), so it is refused to everyone who simply asks.
