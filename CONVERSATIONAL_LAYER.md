# The conversational layer

Doctrine approved by Pierre 2026-10-28 (wording below). Phase 1 is approved subject to gates: the diff inspected, a fast-forward merge only, a controlled deploy with `CONVERSE=off` kept as the rollback, a production smoke test, and observed latency. Phases 2 to 4 are proposed only; each needs its own chat and his yes.

## Why
The app feels transactional; the MCP feels conversational. A message with nothing to act on ("hello", "I want to have a conversation with you") ended as "I didn't catch anything there I could act on." The layer answers such a message in words, using Kimi (already in the stack). No new model, no agent framework, no schema change.

## Doctrine (APPROVED by Pierre, 2026-10-28, in his wording)
> The conversational LLM may converse naturally, explain capabilities, guide onboarding, troubleshoot and narrate information supplied by trusted Office capabilities.
>
> It must not invent business facts, financial figures, calculations, transaction statuses or claims that an operation succeeded. Business facts and financial figures must be grounded in authorised data or deterministic calculations. Any calculations must use trusted, validated inputs and established business rules.
>
> The conversational layer cannot independently create, modify, approve or confirm business records. All business actions remain under the existing Office processing engine and its validation, authorisation, confirmation and audit controls.
>
> The conversational layer may explain what it knows, acknowledge uncertainty, ask clarifying questions and help users express their intentions without forcing every utterance into an actionable task.

Phase 1 uses much less than this allows, on purpose: the model is given no business data at all, so it may state no figure (a reply with a digit is dropped), and onboarding, troubleshooting and narrating trusted data belong to later phases. `OFFICE_CONSTITUTION.md` Principle 2 said the AI has "exactly two seats"; it now carries a dated amendment pointing here, because a bounded third seat (conversation) exists. Nothing else in the constitution changed.

## What Phase 1 does (`tryConverse`)
- **When:** only if the whole reply is the old failure text (`NO_INTENT_MESSAGE`), the intent was "other", and nothing was found, held or refused. A business instruction, a lookup, a follow-up or an upload never reaches it: its path and its records are byte-identical (the test runs one instruction with the layer on and off and compares the reply and every write).
- **What the model gets:** a short Office persona, the capabilities from `REAL_CAPABILITIES` (one per built section of `FEATURES.md`, filtered by the member's permissions), the member's own last six turns (`readHistory`, scope "own", their own business's database), and what they just said. No tools, no business data.
- **What may be shown:** a reply with a digit (any figure, amount, date or count), or a claim that something was recorded, saved, created, sent, confirmed and the like, is dropped and the old message stands. So does an empty or over-long reply, and any failure of the model or the history.
- **Reply:** only `message` changes; envelope version 1, so old APKs work. Typed and voice routes both. Switch: `CONVERSE=off` restores the old behaviour exactly.

## Proof
`tools/converse.test.js` (44 checks, part of the backend suite) and `tools/converse-breakage.js` (24 deliberate breakages, each must fail a test; the first makes a model reply reach a held action). Fragments of one sentence sent in three pieces: each piece is answered as conversation (none gets the failure text), and the third is answered with the first two in view.

## Observed on staging, 2026-10-28 (real Kimi, same code as production; record: `ops/converse-smoke-last.json`, run `40685915830`)
- **Passed:** a greeting ("Morning. What can I do for you?") and "I want to have a conversation with you" ("I'm here. What's on your mind?") get natural, short replies with no figure; a business instruction ("<customer> paid R500") is still held for confirmation (action #149, rejected afterwards, staging left as found); the conversation turns wrote no record and held no action (checked in the history of records written); `CONVERSE` set as a Worker secret turned the layer off at once (the exact old reply) and removing it turned it on again.
- **Limit found, not fixed:** the layer runs only when the pipeline's intent is "other". Real Kimi classifies "What can you help me with?" and "Do you know what I just told you about my day?" as a **lookup**, so they never reach the layer: they got the existing lookup answer ("I don't have that on file", once followed by facts about an invoice). The same happened to one greeting ("Hey Office, how are you"). So "what can you help me with?" does not yet describe capabilities, and a follow-up that uses earlier turns was NOT demonstrated live (it is proven only by the tests, which script the classifier). The two failed smoke checks (`follow_up_uses_the_previous_turn`, `off_samples_all_original_reply`) are this routing, not the layer misbehaving. Options, for Pierre: (a) accept Phase 1 as is; (b) also run the layer when a lookup found nothing ("I don't have that on file"); (c) put a conversational check before the lookup. Each changes Phase 1's gate, so none is built.
- **Latency (n=5 each, wall clock from the runner, includes network):** greeting with the layer on: median 7.1 s, mean 8.5 s (4.4 to 14.3 s); with `CONVERSE` off: median 3.6 s, mean 4.2 s (2.4 to 7.7 s); added by the layer about 3.4 s median, 4.3 s mean. Server-side, the layer's own model call averaged 1.0 s (max 2.9 s, 8 calls, no failures); the existing split and extract calls average 1.6 s and 2.7 s. The wall-clock gap is larger than the call itself and the samples are few and noisy (one "on" sample took the lookup path), so treat it as an order of magnitude: about 1 to 4 s extra. Not optimised.

## Not verified
A smoke test on PRODUCTION (it needs a signed-in session, which only the app has); how the app shows the reply on a device; the follow-up using earlier turns with real Kimi; how to flip `CONVERSE` on production from a phone (on staging a Worker secret named `CONVERSE` set through the Cloudflare API worked; no production workflow for it exists yet).

## Direction change, 2026-10-28 (Pierre): conversation first, like the MCP instance
Pierre wants the native Office to converse the way the connected MCP instance does: a conversation model in front of the engine, the engine (the existing pipeline and the read tools) behind it. The regex gate planned as Option 3 is therefore NOT built; the lookup hardening (live since `23cee53`) is needed either way. The next step is a **staging-only spike** (nothing on production, no writes): the model gets the read tools the connector has, filtered by the member's permissions, plus one tool that hands a real instruction to the existing pipeline unchanged; at most three tool steps per message; a deterministic check that every figure in a reply appears in a tool result; about 30 recorded phrases (greetings, capability and history questions, real lookups, instructions, ambiguous messages, Pierre's two mixed-intent examples); measured: right tool or not, grounded or not, time, model calls. The verdict is one of: Kimi is enough, Kimi needs a stronger model for this seat, or a cheaper gate is still needed. Nothing merges or deploys until Pierre has seen the numbers.

### First valid evaluation of the front door with real Kimi, 2026-10-28 (staging, run `40728863927`, record `ops/converse-spike-last.json`, branch `converse-agent-spike` tip `6d31589`)
The first run (`40723477517`) was INVALID: the front door was not active for most of it (a warm business object kept its old settings), so its "agent" rows were the old pipeline; the evaluation now refuses to run unless the front door is active. That run did show one real danger: asked "Bought diesel for R650" the model chatted back instead of handing off, so work is now recognised in code before any model call (`LOOKS_LIKE_WORK`). Second run, one sample per phrase (28 messages, 23 answered by the front door):
- **Work:** 5 of 5 instructions reached the existing pipeline and were held for confirmation. Nothing was swallowed.
- **Grounding:** no invented figure was seen. Every figure shown came from a tool result. 5 of 23 front-door replies (22%) were replaced by the fixed sentence ("Who are you?", "What can you help me with?", one mixed message, "ok", "what about the other one"); the rejected text was not recorded, so the reason (a digit, a word such as "noted" in the claim check, or length) is not yet known.
- **Talk and history:** 6 of 8 talk phrases and 2 of 2 recall phrases passed; "Do you remember what I just said?" correctly recalled the earlier turn.
- **Reads:** 4 of 8 were answered correctly from the right tool (aged debtors twice, quotes, stock; the debtor figures were right: the staging customer owes R4,000 because the seed runs twice, so the evaluation's expected figures were stale, not the model wrong). 3 of 8 the model asked a clarifying question instead of calling a tool with default arguments (expenses, business performance, upcoming jobs) and 1 gave a wrong "nothing outstanding" after guessing a customer id instead of listing customers first. A mixed message ("what Spike Customer owes, and why debtors are increasing") was answered correctly in 3 model calls, which is the cap.
- **Time (wall clock from the runner, one sample each, noisy):** front door talk median 3.8 s against 8.0 s for the old pipeline; reads 3.0 s against 6.8 s; mixed 3.8 s against 14.4 s; work unchanged (it bypasses the model). Mean model calls per front-door reply 1.3, max 3. One read took 18 s (stock) and one instruction 21 s (the pipeline's own).
- **Verdict:** Kimi is enough for this seat on this evidence, but not yet good enough to ship: the problems are tuning, not capability (log why replies are rejected; tell the model to call a tool with default arguments instead of asking; allow a fourth model call or a customer lookup that needs no id; fix the evaluation's seed), plus a small sample. No stronger model is indicated. A second staging round with about three samples per phrase should follow before any production step.

### Round 2 of the staging evaluation, 2026-10-28 (run `40740153672`, branch `converse-agent-spike` tip `75375fe`, 69 front-door replies, three samples per phrase, a fresh customer each run)
Round 2 changes: the reason a reply is refused is recorded, the persona says to call a tool with default arguments and to find a customer with `list_customers` instead of guessing an id, four model calls are allowed, the seed no longer accumulates. Results:
- **Work:** 5 of 5 instructions handed to the existing pipeline and held again; none swallowed.
- **Tool choice (reads and mixed, 30 attempts):** the right tool was called 19 times (63%, up from 50%); the model asked a question instead 7 times (23%, down from 38%); 4 times no tool and a refused reply. No attempt used the fourth model call (max 3 calls; 48 replies took 1, 14 took 2, 7 took 3).
- **The guard is now the main problem, not the model:** 18 of 69 replies (26%) were replaced by the fixed sentence (figure 9, claim 7, length 2), and 9 of the 19 tool-using replies were refused despite the right tool. Examples of correct answers refused: "owes R2,000 ... with R1,000 paid against it" and "None of them have a scheduled date yet" (the claim check is a blunt word list: "paid", "scheduled" are legitimate in a reading); a capability list over 700 characters. Some refusals are right: the model summed three R850 expenses to R2,550 (a calculation, refused as doctrine says) and wrote R9,308.00 for a figure the tool gave as 930800 (a unit slip). Answered correctly with the right tool and a grounded figure: 9 of 30.
- **Regression:** "Do you remember what I just said?" 0 of 3 (it said it keeps no memory of the chat) although it recalled correctly in round 1; the earlier turns are in its context, so the persona needs to say so.
- **Time (runner wall clock):** front-door replies median 2.9 s, mean 5.7 s, 90th percentile 10.9 s; talk 2.3 s against 6.2 s for the old pipeline; reads 4.2 s against 4.3 s (parity); mixed 8.6 s against 6.9 s (slower); work unchanged. Tail outliers of 40 to 45 s on two reads (profit and loss; a three-step customer read) need a time budget.
- **Not ready to ship.** Next round (staging only): narrow the claim check to claims that THIS conversation did something ("I've recorded", "has been saved", "done"), not words in a reading; raise the agent's length limit to 1,200; record which figures were ungrounded; tell the model its earlier turns are visible; add a time budget that returns the fixed sentence. Expectation, not a measurement: most of the 9 wrongly refused reads would pass.

### Round 3 of the staging evaluation, 2026-10-28 (run `40758073176`, branch `converse-agent-spike` tip `fb8a48f`, 69 front-door replies, three samples per phrase)
Round 3 changes: a claim check about who did what (first person, "has been saved", "that is confirmed", bare "done"; a reading such as "R1,000 paid against it" passes), a 1,200 character limit, ungrounded figures named in the refusal, a persona line that earlier turns are visible, a 25 second time budget. Against round 2:
- **Talk and history are ready on staging:** talk 24 of 24 (was 20 of 24), recall 6 of 6 (was 3 of 6; "Do you remember what I just said?" now 3 of 3), 0 refusals in either. Instructions: 5 of 5 handed to the existing pipeline and held.
- **Refusals fell from 26% to 16%** (11 of 69: figure 10, claim 1; length 0, time 0). **Speed:** front-door median 2.5 s (was 2.9), mean 3.5 s (was 5.7), 90th percentile 7.7 s (was 10.9), slowest 20.6 s (was 44.6); talk 2.7 s against 5.6 s for the old pipeline, reads 3.6 s against 5.6 s, mixed 4.0 s against 8.7 s. One reply used all four model calls.
- **Reads are not ready:** right tool 17 of 30 (57%); the model asked a question instead of calling a tool 9 times (30%, the persona line did not fix it: "what jobs are coming up" 3 of 3, expenses and business performance mostly); correct, grounded and shown 10 of 30 (33%); refused despite the right tool 6 of 17.
- **What the named figures show:** several refusals are correct under the doctrine because the model derived a figure (a total of R15,227 over the debtors list, R3,400 as four R850 expenses, "10 items in stock"). A second group is unexplained and may be a unit problem: for the load-test customers the model wrote R9,308.00, R4,654.00 and R1,185.00 where the old pipeline had written R930,800 and R465,400 for the same customers earlier; one of the two is 100 times off. Nothing in `mcp.ts` converts to cents, so this is UNRESOLVED and must be checked in the tool output before any amount from either path is trusted. A third group (a quote list of R3,000 and R2,000, an invoice number 14111) holds figures that exist in staging but were not in the tool results the guard saw: possibly results cut at 8,000 characters, possibly figures reused from an earlier turn.
- **Proposed round 4 (staging only, needs Pierre's go):** (1) when a reply is refused ONLY for an ungrounded figure, hand the original message to the existing lookup (the authorised, hardened path) instead of showing the fixed sentence; claim, length and time refusals keep the fixed sentence; (2) put today's date in the persona and tell the model to use the current month when a period is needed and say so; (3) tell it never to reuse a figure from an earlier turn; (4) record a short sample of each tool result and whether it was cut, so the unexplained figures can be traced. Expectation, not a measurement: most refused reads would be answered by the old lookup, at about 3 s extra.
- **Not ready for production.** A smaller first step is possible later (talk, recall and hand-off only, reads left to the old pipeline) but is not proposed or built.
