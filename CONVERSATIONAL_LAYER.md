# The conversational layer

Doctrine approved by Pierre 2026-10-28 (wording below). Phase 1 is approved subject to gates: the diff inspected, a fast-forward merge only, a controlled deploy with `CONVERSE=off` kept as the rollback, a production smoke test, and observed latency. Phases 2 to 4 are proposed only; each needs its own chat and his yes.

## Why
The app feels transactional; the MCP feels conversational. A message with nothing to act on ("hello", "I want to have a conversation with you") ended as "I didn't catch anything there I could act on." The layer answers such a message in words, using Kimi (already in the stack). No new model, no agent framework, no schema change.

## Doctrine (APPROVED by Pierre, 2026-10-28, in his wording)
> The conversational LLM may converse naturally, explain capabilities, guide onboarding, troubleshoot and narrate information supplied by trusted Office capabilities.
>
> It must not invent business facts, financial figures, calculations, transaction statuses or claims that an operation succeeded. Business facts and financial figures must be grounded in authorised data or deterministic calculations. Any calculations must use trusted, validated inputs and established business rules.
>
> The conversational layer cannot independently create, modify, approve or confirm business records. All business actions remain under the existing Office processing engine and its validation, authorisation, confirmation and audit controls.
>
> The conversational layer may explain what it knows, acknowledge uncertainty, ask clarifying questions and help users express their intentions without forcing every utterance into an actionable task.

Phase 1 uses much less than this allows, on purpose: the model is given no business data at all, so it may state no figure (a reply with a digit is dropped), and onboarding, troubleshooting and narrating trusted data belong to later phases. `OFFICE_CONSTITUTION.md` Principle 2 said the AI has "exactly two seats"; it now carries a dated amendment pointing here, because a bounded third seat (conversation) exists. Nothing else in the constitution changed.

## What Phase 1 does (`tryConverse`)
- **When:** only if the whole reply is the old failure text (`NO_INTENT_MESSAGE`), the intent was "other", and nothing was found, held or refused. A business instruction, a lookup, a follow-up or an upload never reaches it: its path and its records are byte-identical (the test runs one instruction with the layer on and off and compares the reply and every write).
- **What the model gets:** a short Office persona, the capabilities from `REAL_CAPABILITIES` (one per built section of `FEATURES.md`, filtered by the member's permissions), the member's own last six turns (`readHistory`, scope "own", their own business's database), and what they just said. No tools, no business data.
- **What may be shown:** a reply with a digit (any figure, amount, date or count), or a claim that something was recorded, saved, created, sent, confirmed and the like, is dropped and the old message stands. So does an empty or over-long reply, and any failure of the model or the history.
- **Reply:** only `message` changes; envelope version 1, so old APKs work. Typed and voice routes both. Switch: `CONVERSE=off` restores the old behaviour exactly.

## Proof
`tools/converse.test.js` (44 checks, part of the backend suite) and `tools/converse-breakage.js` (24 deliberate breakages, each must fail a test; the first makes a model reply reach a held action). Fragments of one sentence sent in three pieces: each piece is answered as conversation (none gets the failure text), and the third is answered with the first two in view.

## Observed on staging, 2026-10-28 (real Kimi, same code as production; record: `ops/converse-smoke-last.json`, run `40685915830`)
- **Passed:** a greeting ("Morning. What can I do for you?") and "I want to have a conversation with you" ("I'm here. What's on your mind?") get natural, short replies with no figure; a business instruction ("<customer> paid R500") is still held for confirmation (action #149, rejected afterwards, staging left as found); the conversation turns wrote no record and held no action (checked in the history of records written); `CONVERSE` set as a Worker secret turned the layer off at once (the exact old reply) and removing it turned it on again.
- **Limit found, not fixed:** the layer runs only when the pipeline's intent is "other". Real Kimi classifies "What can you help me with?" and "Do you know what I just told you about my day?" as a **lookup**, so they never reach the layer: they got the existing lookup answer ("I don't have that on file", once followed by facts about an invoice). The same happened to one greeting ("Hey Office, how are you"). So "what can you help me with?" does not yet describe capabilities, and a follow-up that uses earlier turns was NOT demonstrated live (it is proven only by the tests, which script the classifier). The two failed smoke checks (`follow_up_uses_the_previous_turn`, `off_samples_all_original_reply`) are this routing, not the layer misbehaving. Options, for Pierre: (a) accept Phase 1 as is; (b) also run the layer when a lookup found nothing ("I don't have that on file"); (c) put a conversational check before the lookup. Each changes Phase 1's gate, so none is built.
- **Latency (n=5 each, wall clock from the runner, includes network):** greeting with the layer on: median 7.1 s, mean 8.5 s (4.4 to 14.3 s); with `CONVERSE` off: median 3.6 s, mean 4.2 s (2.4 to 7.7 s); added by the layer about 3.4 s median, 4.3 s mean. Server-side, the layer's own model call averaged 1.0 s (max 2.9 s, 8 calls, no failures); the existing split and extract calls average 1.6 s and 2.7 s. The wall-clock gap is larger than the call itself and the samples are few and noisy (one "on" sample took the lookup path), so treat it as an order of magnitude: about 1 to 4 s extra. Not optimised.

## Direction change, 2026-10-28 (Pierre): conversation first, like the MCP instance
Pierre wants the native Office to converse the way the connected MCP instance does: a conversation model in front of the engine, the engine (the existing pipeline and the read tools) behind it. The regex gate planned as Option 3 is therefore NOT built; the lookup hardening (live since `23cee53`) is needed either way. The next step is a **staging-only spike** (nothing on production, no writes): the model gets the read tools the connector has, filtered by the member's permissions, plus one tool that hands a real instruction to the existing pipeline unchanged; at most three tool steps per message; a deterministic check that every figure in a reply appears in a tool result; about 30 recorded phrases (greetings, capability and history questions, real lookups, instructions, ambiguous messages, Pierre's two mixed-intent examples); measured: right tool or not, grounded or not, time, model calls. The verdict is one of: Kimi is enough, Kimi needs a stronger model for this seat, or a cheaper gate is still needed. Nothing merges or deploys until Pierre has seen the numbers.

### Drill-down: where the front door still falls short of the connected MCP instance (2026-10-28, from the round 3 record)
Of 30 read attempts, 10 were correct and shown. The other 20 by cause: **9 the model asked instead of calling a tool**, although `list_jobs`, `list_expenses` and `get_profit_and_loss` have NO required arguments (only `get_customer_receivables` needs an id and `analyse` a topic and period), so the questions were unnecessary; **9 the right tool was called but the reply held figures that were not in the result** (sums, counts, the divide-by-100 slip, a few unexplained); 1 a wrong "nothing outstanding"; 1 a refusal by the claim check. 18 of the 20 are the model not following explicit rules (call with defaults, copy figures exactly, never calculate), so Pierre's reading that the model is the main gap holds for reads; the guard, now narrowed, was right to refuse what it refused. The connected MCP instance follows those same rules from the same instructions, which is the difference. Options: (A) the model only talks and routes, and every business question goes to the existing lookup (the proven path, no model-composed figures; loses multi-step reasoning such as "why are debtors increasing"); (B) the model narrates and code renders the figures from the tool result beside it (no figure error by construction; needs a renderer per tool); (C) a model bake-off with the same harness and phrases, one variable changed (Kimi against another Workers AI model, and against Claude through the API if Pierre provides a key, which also sends business data to Anthropic). (C) is the decisive experiment; (A) is the safe fallback either way.

### Moonshot as the conversation layer: first matrix, 2026-10-29 (run `40821456146`, staging at `23342e8`, record `ops/converse-moonshot-matrix-last.json`, 28 phrases at two samples, 8 configurations)

Policy A: the person gets the engine's words verbatim. Policy B: the engine's read answer is also sent so the model can narrate it, with a figures and claims check. Kimi models the key can call: `kimi-k2.6`, `kimi-k2.7-code`, `kimi-k2.7-code-highspeed`, `kimi-k3`, `kimi-k3.1-pro`, `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed`. Results (talk and recall judged as answered by the conversation layer; reads judged as routed to the engine with a real answer):
- **The engine already answers reads:** the old pipeline answered 10 of 10 read and mixed phrases (median 3.7 s). So the front door's job is routing and natural talk, not retrieval.
- **Policy A, per model:** `kimi-k2.6` thinking ON routed 20 of 20 reads and mixed (reads 16/16, mixed 4/4, talk 16/16, recall 4/4, work 5/5), total median 5.9 s, p90 11.1 s, 3.6 s per Moonshot call; `kimi-k2.7-code` routed 18 of 20, total median 3.0 s, p90 8.5 s; `kimi-k2.7-code-highspeed` routed 16 of 20, total median 1.4 s, p90 5.7 s, 0.9 s per call; `kimi-k2.6` thinking OFF routed 9 of 20, total median 1.2 s. Instructions 5 of 5 held in every configuration (they never reach the model).
- **A flaw in this design, found in the record, polluted the non-thinking results:** when the model answered a read itself, its reply was the literal placeholder text "(The Office answered that from the business records.)" that the history uses for earlier engine answers: the model copied it. 11 of the 20 `kimi-k2.6` thinking-off reads and 2 to 7 in the K2.7 and Policy B runs. The thinking models did not copy it. Fix before any conclusion: do not show earlier engine turns as assistant replies; fold them into one system note listing the person's words.
- **Policy B is not worth it on this evidence:** narration was accepted for 9 to 11 of the engine routes, the examples were faithful, but time rose (`kimi-k2.6` thinking on: 23 s median for a narrated read, p90 38 s; thinking off 7.4 s) and the engine's answers cross to Moonshot. Policy A keeps the engine's words verbatim and sends nothing from the engine.
- **Unexplained:** `kimi-k2.7-code-highspeed` under Policy B had 10 messages with no routing (Moonshot call failed or timed out, 7 to 17 s each), none under Policy A; the HTTP status was not recorded, so a rate limit cannot be told from a timeout.
- **Tokens (46 calls):** about 37,600 to 40,000 in for every configuration (the persona and the tool, roughly 820 per call); out 877 (K2.6 off), 2,300 to 2,400 (K2.7), 7,300 (K2.6 on), up to 14,900 for K2.6 on under Policy B.

### Office-side diagnosis: the baseline from run 2, 2026-10-29 (150 read and mixed attempts: 30 messages in each of the five configurations that routed correctly; the same Office code answered all of them)
The router is not the problem; this separates what the Office returned into what it actually was. Every row is in `ops/converse-moonshot-matrix-run2.json`.
- **"How much does Spike Alder Birch owe?" (15 of 15 judged wrong, actually correct):** every reply said the customer owes R4000, invoiced R6000, paid R2000, consistent with "Show me ... outstanding invoices". My matrix hard-coded the expected figures as R2000 or R3000; the staging data held double that, so the EXPECTATION was stale, not the Office. A judge that hard-codes figures is a method flaw; ground truth must be read from the Office's own data in the same run.
- **Opened a dashboard (45 of 150):** "Who owes us money?", "How much have we spent on expenses?" and "How is the business doing financially?" each returned the marker `__OPEN_DASHBOARD__:aged_debtors` or `...:financial_snapshot`, which the app turns into a screen. By design, not a failure, but it is not a spoken answer: a voice or text-only person gets no words. My judge counted these as right only because no figure was expected.
- **Wrong, a failure of the Office (30 of 150): "Do we have any open quotes?" and "What do we have in stock?"** both returned "Did you want to see the full financial snapshot, or are you asking specifically about who owes you money?". Stage: `classifyDashboardIntent` in `intents/lookup.ts` knows only three labels (financial snapshot, aged debtors, unsure) for a business-wide question, so anything outside money, such as quotes, stock or jobs, comes back "unsure" and gets that question. This is a missing capability, not a model slip: the connected MCP instance answers quotes, stock and jobs with its read tools (`list_quotes`, `get_stock`, `list_jobs`), and the staging front-door spikes answered them through those tools. The in-app lookup does not, which is exactly where the Office falls short of the MCP instance.
- **"What jobs do I have coming up?" (15):** "I don't have any information about upcoming jobs on file." (7), "I don't have that on file." (3, judged wrong by my regex), and 2 replies that went on to list unrelated facts ("The facts I have are about open tasks to call Thandi, confirmed invoices and payments..."). Whether the staging business has any upcoming job was NOT established, so "no information" cannot yet be called right or wrong. The unrelated-facts replies are a gap in the lookup hardening of 2026-10-28: it only cuts a reply that begins with the exact words "I don't have that on file", so a rewording slips through.
- **Mixed, second half unanswered (15 of 15):** "tell me what X owes and then help me understand why our debtors are increasing" got the owes figure and then a stock sentence about expenses linked to the job, but nothing about why.
